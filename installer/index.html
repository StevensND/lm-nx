LM-NX Installer

Builds the Nintendo Switch package of Luigi's Mansion from your own GameCube disc. Everything runs in this browser: nothing is uploaded.
The port is based on Luigi's Mansion Recompiled, the recompilation made by rob646464.

3. Copy it to the Switch

  1. Extract the downloaded .zip file and copy the LM-NX folder inside it into sdmc:/switch/ on your SD card. When you update, let it replace the files that are already there.
  2. Start LM-NX.nro from the Homebrew Menu in title takeover mode (launch a game while holding R to open the Homebrew Menu) or create a forwarder with Sphaira.
  3. The shaders are already in the package: the first start only loads them, it does not compile them.

What you need

  • A Nintendo Switch that can run homebrew, with custom firmware such as Atmosphère.
  • Your own copy of Luigi's Mansion (USA) for GameCube, dumped from your disc. The European disc (first release) is optional: it adds the Hidden Mansion and four more languages.
  • Free space on the SD card: about 1.7 GB with the USA disc, about 3.2 GB with both discs. Your computer needs the same space for the zip.
  • A computer with an up-to-date browser to make the package.

How to start it

Start LM-NX.nro in title takeover mode: hold R while you launch any game and the Homebrew Menu opens with all the memory of the console. From the Album the game does not have enough memory.

The Switch controller works as a GameCube controller:

Settings menu: press both sticks at once (L3 + R3). There you can change the graphics, the sound, the controls and the game options. Every button can be remapped, and the sticks have dead zone, sensitivity and invert options. Your changes are saved when the menu closes.

Why do I need my own disc?

This page does not host or send any part of the game: it builds the package from the disc you own, inside your browser.

Which discs work?

Luigi's Mansion (USA), GLME01, is required. The European disc, GLMP01, is optional and only its first release (revision 0) works. Other releases are not supported.

Can I use .rvz, .wia, .gcz or .ciso images?

Yes: they become the full .iso while the package is made. Trimmed images such as .nkit.iso do not work.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Your discs are read only in your browser and the zip is saved straight to your computer.

Where are my saves?

In sdmc:/switch/LM-NX/guardados/. Copy that folder to keep a backup.

How do I update?

Make lm-nx-update.zip on this page and copy its LM-NX folder over the old one. Your discs and your saves stay as they are.

What are the shaders?

The game needs about 2,000 shaders, small programs for the GPU. This page compiles them on your computer while it makes the package and puts them in cache/, so the first start only loads them, in a few seconds.

Why is my console compiling shaders?

It happens once, with a progress bar, when the cache/ folder is missing or damaged, or when you updated LM-NX.nro by hand instead of with lm-nx-update.zip. The shaders are saved and later starts load them in seconds.

The game closes or the screen stays black when it starts

Check that the folder is sdmc:/switch/LM-NX/ with LM-NX.nro inside, and start it in title takeover mode (hold R while you launch a game), not from the Album.

The game cannot find the disc

The LM-NX folder must have orig/GLME01.iso inside. If it is missing, make lm-nx.zip again and copy the whole folder.

The page does not accept my disc

It needs a full dump or an .rvz, .wia, .gcz or .ciso made from one. Trimmed images and the European revision 1 do not work.

The first start takes a long time

The shaders come ready in the package. If they are missing, the console compiles them on the first start, which takes longer only that time.

Something else? See Report a bug.

Found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include:

  • The version of LM-NX you installed, for example 1.0.0.
  • Whether you play docked or in handheld mode.
  • What you were doing just before the problem, and whether it happens again.
  • The files in sdmc:/switch/LM-NX/logs/.
  • A photo or a video, if it is something you can see or hear.
